are made available under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

When reusing them, please attribute:

> Data: CASP Tracker (casptracker.eu), based on the ESMA MiCA interim registers.

## Raw ESMA snapshots (`source/`)

The CSV files in `source/` are unmodified snapshots of the interim MiCA
registers published by the European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA)
at esma.europa.eu. They are (c) ESMA and are reproduced here with source
acknowledgment, in line with ESMA's legal notice, which authorises
reproduction provided the source is acknowledged.

Official register page:
<https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica>
